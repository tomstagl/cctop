Button is the only pressable leaf, and its hover prop applies label styles “while the nearest enclosing keyed Box, or given a scope its group, is hovered”. So a cell is a keyed Box holding two or three Buttons that share one scope and one onPress: the whole area lights together, any part of it clicks, and each part keeps its own colour — which one Button with one label could not do.
The digit is not drawn by cctop. plain means “the hotkey in the accent color, a colon, the label (1: Yes)”, so 1:ctx 35% 616k left is the engine's own rendering of a hotkey — native affordance, no invention, and it follows the person's theme.
