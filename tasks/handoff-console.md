# Hand-off: implementing Console (dashboard v2)

The contracts are `tasks/prd-cctop-dashboard-v2.md` v2.0 (what and why) and
`tasks/plan-dashboard-v2.md` (how, in order, with every file:line). This file is
only the trigger.

## How to run it

**One part per session.** Paste the prompt below, changing `Part A` to `Part B`
or `Part C` for the later runs. Do not run them in one session: Part A is four
independent commits on today's layout, Part B is a single push across two
surfaces with a public schema bump in it, and Part C is mostly prose plus an
asset step that needs a machine with Chrome, ffmpeg and cwebp.

| part | plan | ships | needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | §2 (A1–A4) | the cost bug, six honest figures, the ramp and the glyph fix | nothing |
| **B** | §3 (B0–B2) | Console on both surfaces, schema 2, the tiles deleted | A4 landed; the two decisions below |
| **C** | §3 (B3–B4) | the key map, README, site, guide, demo assets | B2 landed |

Part A is worth shipping whether or not B happens. A4 gates B2: the pane has no
colour ramp (PRD §3.6), so the `stacked_bar` glyph fix is the only thing keeping
its context bar readable.

## Two decisions the author owns

Both are deliberately absent from the PRD — see its §9 — and both must be
settled **before** Part B writes the schema, because `Cell.key` is a wire field
and deciding late costs a schema 3:

1. **`0` or `Esc` as the way home.** Both are wired in the prototype.
2. **Are six cells the right six?** They are the four budgets plus health plus
   volume. If one turns out never to be pressed it should become a line inside
   another body rather than a cell of its own.
