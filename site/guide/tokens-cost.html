Tokens & Cost

Where every token went — cache read, cache write, fresh input, output and thinking — and what the session costs, live.

Cache read

reads as
cache read ▇▇▇▇▇▇▇ 1.62 M
What it means
The cheap tokens — read at roughly a tenth of the input price. The bigger this is as a share of the total, the less the session costs.
What to do
Read this next to the cache-hit ratio, not on its own — it's the numerator, not the whole health check.
Measured
tokens · from the transcript · exact · how

Cache write

reads as
cache write ▇▇▇ 148k
What it means
Written at 1.25×–2× the input price, and it happens whenever the prefix changed or the cache entry expired.
What to do
A big or repeated cache-write bill points straight at cache misses or cache expiry — check the coach.

Fresh input

reads as
fresh in ▇ 24k
What it means
Uncached tokens billed in full, almost always a large paste or genuinely new content introduced this turn.
What to do
A spike here is usually a paste that could have been a file reference instead.

Cache hit ratio

reads as
cache hit 90 %
What it means
The single best proxy for whether this session is using prompt caching well. Green from 80 %, amber from 50 %, red below.
What to do
Sustained below 60 %? Something in the prefix is changing every turn — a hook or status line printing the time is the usual suspect.

Cache TTL

reads as
cache TTL 1h
What it means
Your real budget for how long you can pause mid-session before the next request pays a full cold rewrite.
What to do
Pace batched questions to land inside this window rather than trickling them out past it.

Cost

reads as
≈ $4.37
What it means
What this session would cost at API list price, even on a subscription plan with no per-token bill.
What to do
Watch it alongside burn rate if you're trying to keep a session inside a budget.
Measured
USD · from Claude Code's own cost record · the transcript · the price table · ≈ when any part of it is estimated · how

Combined cost

What it means
The session's whole spend — Claude Code's own ledger, which already holds the subagents' earlier calls, plus what cctop priced after it on both the main transcript and the agents. Panel 2's headline.
What to do
Read the breakdown line under it: `main`/`ledger` and `since` add up to the headline, `agents` says how much of it went to agents. `a` drops them from the figure.
Advisor
A48 Agent waste · A14 Subagent model choice

Cost by model

What it means
Splits spend by model — the number that matters once subagents are running on something other than the main thread's model.
What to do
If a cheap search or summarise task is running on your priciest model, point that subagent at a smaller one.

Cost per call

What it means
context × cache-read price + median output × output price, at the current context; at the cache-write price of the observed TTL when the cache is cold
Measured
USD · from the transcript · the status line (after cctop install) · the price table · always an estimate, priced from the list table · how

Cost per turn

What it means
cost per call × the session's own median calls per turn (turns with ≥ 1 call), also given at 100 k of context; next 30 calls = cost per call × 30
Measured
USD · from the transcript · the price table · always an estimate, priced from the list table · how

Team cost

What it means
Σ over the teammates of the team this session leads, each from its own transcript: its cost-state.totalCostUSD where one exists, plus its priced calls after that line (all of them while it has none); members found from ~/.claude/teams/<team>/config.json, the lead's teammate_spawned results and a scan of the transcripts naming the team (teamName == session-<lead id8>). Part of cost_combined, toggled with the agents by a

USD · from D12 · D2c · Claude Code's own cost record · the price table · ≈ for a teammate's calls after its last cost-state, or all of them while it runs; ≈ and "N of M read" when a transcript is missing · how

Limit weight

What it means

Measured

Behaviour flags

What it means
/usage's five flags as shares of the weighted usage: cache_miss (requests with > 100 k uncached tokens), long_context (> 150 k context), subagent_heavy, high_parallel (≥ 4 live sessions), cron (active ≥ 8 h); shown with Claude Code's own tip text at ≥ 10 %
Measured
% · from the transcript · the session list · D12 · exact · how
