2 Tokens & Cost

Where every token went — cache read, cache write, fresh input, output and thinking — and what the session costs, live.

Cache read

cache_read · tokens

Why it mattersThe cheap tokens — read at roughly a tenth of the input price. The bigger this is as a share of the total, the less the session costs.
What to doRead this next to cache hit ratio, not on its own — it's the numerator, not the whole health check.

Cache write

cache_write · tokens

Why it mattersWritten at 1.25×–2× the input price, and it happens whenever the prefix changed or the cache entry expired.
What to doA big or repeated cache-write bill points straight at cache misses or cache expiry — check the Advisor. Advisor: A01 Cache misses · A02 Cache expiry

Fresh input

fresh_input · tokens

Why it mattersUncached tokens billed in full, almost always a large paste or genuinely new content introduced this turn.
What to doA spike here is usually a paste that could have been a file reference instead. Advisor: A11 Pasted input

Output

output · tokens

Why it mattersWhat you pay for the model's replies, thinking tokens included.
What to doCompare against thinking to see how much of the reply is reasoning versus visible text.

Thinking

thinking · tokens

Why it mattersBilled as output but never shown as text — a silent cost that's easy to miss.
What to doA high thinking share on routine edits is a sign to drop the effort level for that kind of work. Advisor: A08 Thinking share

Cache hit ratio

cache_hit_ratio · ratio

Why it mattersThe single best proxy for whether this session is using prompt caching well.
What to doSustained below 60%? Something in the prefix is changing every turn — a hook or status line injecting the time is the usual suspect. Advisor: A01 Cache misses

Cache TTL

cache_ttl · enum

Why it mattersYour real budget for how long you can pause mid-session before the next request pays a full cold rewrite.
What to doPace batched questions to land inside this window rather than trickling them out past it. Advisor: A02 Cache expiry

Cost

cost · USD

Why it mattersWhat this session would cost at API list price, even on a subscription plan with no per-token bill.
What to doWatch it alongside burn rate if you're trying to keep a session inside a budget.

Cost by model

cost_by_model · USD

Why it mattersSplits spend by model — the number that matters once subagents are running on something other than the main thread's model.
What to doIf a cheap search/summarize task is running on your priciest model, point that subagent at a smaller one. Advisor: A14 Subagent model choice

Burn rate

burn_rate · USD/h

Why it mattersAn early-warning $/hour figure extrapolated from the last 15 minutes, well before the final bill or a usage limit surprises you.
What to doA sudden jump is the moment to decide whether to keep going at this rate or change course. Advisor: A13 Rate-limit pacing

Input rate

input_rate · tokens/min

Why it mattersA proxy for how chatty the session is — tokens per minute sent to the API.
What to doMany tiny prompts cost more in aggregate than one prompt that bundles the same instructions. Advisor: A12 Chatty turns
